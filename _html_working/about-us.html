We Are Revolutionary Front

We are a political organization within DSA's DFW chapters. We exist to heighten working-class consciousness within the DFW Metroplex through education, agitation, mass rallies, direct action, and grassroots organizing. By doing so, we will assist our fellow workers and oppressed community members in developing the knowledge, desire, and institutions necessary to bring forth liberation.

Our primary ideological commitments are to revolutionary socialism and anti-imperialism. "Revolutionary socialism" refers to the project aimed at abolishing the capitalist system and replacing it with a workers state, which would restructure the economy along collectivist lines. "Anti-imperialism" refers to the movement opposing the neocolonial world order and its profit-driven war machine. These commitments represent two halves of a single mission: freeing the planet from the tyrannical rule of the imperialist owning class.

Our primary organizing principle is that of democratic centralism within the Marxist-Leninist tradition. As such, we understand that each member, or cadre, is a professional revolutionary, one who has the responsibility, commitment, and capacity to carry on the political tasks necessary to advance the class struggle.

Our membership largely consists of seasoned activists looking to build a new political home.

Our experiences have taught us that democracy, accountability, and transparency are paramount. To that end, our formation combines tight discipline with broad rank-and-file participation. Our leadership is beholden to the decisions made by the members who elected them. They are to faithfully carry out assigned tasks and align themselves with the will of the majority.

As a left-wing organization, we will contribute to the fight for a more radical DSA. But as Marxist-Leninists, our primary focus will never stray from publicly-facing, mass-oriented activity. The frontlines of the revolution are everywhere, and we intend to occupy each of them.

We are Revolutionary Front. We are for a communist future, and against this imperialist reality. If you feel the same way, then we look forward to seeing you in the struggle.
