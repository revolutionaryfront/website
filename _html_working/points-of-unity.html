Points of Unity

The primary goals guiding all of
Revolutionary Front's work are:

  • 1.Abolishing capitalism
    and imperialism
  • 2.Advancing the revolutionary struggle
    towards socialism
  • 3.Supporting all struggles against
    capitalism and imperialism
1.Abolishing capitalism
and imperialism

RF understands capitalism as:
An economic system characterized by 1) generalized commodity production and exchange, 2) private ownership of the means of production, and 3) the exploitation of labor-power by property owners to generate profit.

Capitalism features two main classes: the capitalist class, also known as the bourgeoisie, and the working class, also known as the proletariat. The bourgeoisie acquires its wealth by paying workers less than the value they create with their labor-power. This produces tension between both classes, which the bourgeoisie manages through a special apparatus of coercion. That apparatus is called the state.

To maintain itself, the capitalist state has set up a plethora of repressive institutions, which operate both domestically and abroad. Examples include local law enforcement, the CIA, the FBI, ICE, the military, etc. These violent arms of the state exist to uphold the deeply unequal class relations inherent within all capitalist societies.

Additionally, the capitalist state excludes working people from having any meaningful participation or agency over the direction of their societies. So-called "liberal democracies" may provide a facade of popular engagement, but real power always remains with the wealthy elite, who manipulate political outcomes with their connections and resources.

Furthermore, capitalism engenders various forms of "special oppression" (e.g. oppression based on race, gender, sexual orientation, nationality, ability, age, etc.). By dividing the proletariat along such lines, capitalists can justify exploiting certain workers at a higher level, thereby securing superprofits. Also, these "special oppressions" prevent class-wide unity by pitting workers against one another.

RF understands imperialism as:
The highest stage of capitalism, in which free competition gives way to concentration, monopoly, and a financial oligarchy. At this level of development, capitalism outgrows its national boundaries and seeks to control foreign economies, leaving poverty, war, destruction, and death in its wake.

Since the end of WW2, the United States has reigned as the imperial hegemon, with Western Europe, Japan, etc. serving as its loyal satellites. Therefore, communists boast a particular obligation to oppose this country's aggression on all fronts. The international working class has a common interest in defeating this behemoth. Revolutionary Front stands in unwavering solidarity with all those struggling against imperialist subjugation.

We must abolish this system because:
For too long, the imperialist beast has run rampant around the globe, feasting upon millions of lives to sustain itself. The time has come for people with revolutionary class consciousness to bring this carnage to an end, before it destroys the entire planet.

2.Advancing the revolutionary struggle
towards socialism

RF understands socialism as:
An economic system characterized by 1) production for use instead of profit, 2) public ownership of the means of production, and 3) the rational allocation of resources according to a common plan.

To establish socialism, we must depose the capitalist state and replace it with a dictatorship of the proletariat. This government shall expropriate all property from the capitalists and place it in the collective hands of the workers, who will democratically manage the wealth of society.

The socialist system will grant the right to self-determination to all victims of the capitalist-imperialist beast. The resources the state possesses will be devoted to the international working class and the betterment of human existence.

The most important weapon in the struggle for socialism is the science of Marxism-Leninism.

RF understands Marxism as:
A scientific method for analyzing society and making revolution. The three pillars of Marxism are dialectical materialism, historical materialism, and the critique of political economy.

RF understands Marxism-Leninism as:
Marxism adapted for the highest stage of capitalism. The three pillars of Leninism are the theory of imperialism, the theory of the Party, and the theory of proletarian revolution. Marxism-Leninism provides us with guidance on how the working class may achieve revolution and successfully defend the nascent workers state from the forces of reaction.

RF supports revolution instead of reformism because:
The capitalist state is designed to prevent the working class from wielding power. The major parties are controlled by corporate interests. The bureaucracy is in bed with private industries. And the military can be deployed to safeguard property relations at a moment's notice. Any attempts to use the existing state for socialist purposes are doomed for failure. It cannot merely be reformed; it must be smashed.

While campaigns for progressive legislation or elected office can help raise consciousness and build organizational capacity, they must be incorporated into a broader revolutionary program. Otherwise, they will produce, at best, a social democracy, which will continue to prey off the global south, and will be unable to even guarantee the long-term stability of its own social welfare programs in the future, as shown by the introduction of austerity into the European social democracies.

RF rejects "entryism" within the Democratic Party because:
The Democratic Party will not shift its objectives because enough "socialists" achieve electoral victories under its umbrella. On the contrary: to successfully run as Democrats, socialists must sand off their "rough edges," such that they become virtually indistinguishable from left-liberals once in office.

In other words: socialists cannot change the Democratic Party, but the Democratic Party will change them.

We recognize this party to be one where social movements go to die. The Democrats, like the Republicans, are a party of the capitalist class. While these factions have their differences, they both support the exploitation and plunder of the majority of the world's population for the benefit of a small class of individuals.

RF rejects "patriotic" approaches to socialism because:
The history of the U.S. is covered in blood.

The United States was founded on the genocide of the Indigenous peoples and slavery of African people. Westward expansion necessitated the genocide of millions, the destruction of many cultures, and the eradication of nature, all for the purpose of profit. After the U.S. conquered the entire continent, it soon expanded overseas, by pillaging Hawai'i, Puerto Rico, and the Philippines. From there on, it slowly rose until World War 2, when it became the clear leader of the imperialist nations, setting off on a path of destruction that has largely remained unchallenged.

There's nothing "progressive" to salvage from this track record. Even the Civil War was primarily fought for economic, rather than moral, reasons. Consequently, any "socialism" that professes U.S. patriotism is just reaction by another name.

3.Supporting all struggles against
capitalism and imperialism

RF offers its unwavering solidarity to all of the socialist states across the world, past and present.

We uphold and publicly support the current socialist projects which exist in the People's Republic of China, the Democratic People's Republic of Korea, the Republic of Cuba, the Socialist Republic of Vietnam, and the Lao People's Democratic Republic. As we go about our journey building socialism in the U.S., we must draw lessons from these countries and creatively apply them to our material conditions.

Additionally, we uphold and publicly support all former socialist projects, such as those that existed in the Soviet Union and the Eastern Bloc. This means we defend the legacy of the October Revolution and its primary stewards, Vladimir Lenin and Joseph Stalin. We reject Trotsky's falsifications, which exaggerate Stalin's faults while erasing his role in defeating the Nazi menace and presiding over the greatest period of Soviet development.

RF believes that self-determination is an essential right which must be possessed by all people's of the world.

Every nation must be allowed to chart its own destiny in peace. We stand with the victims of America's "regime change" wars, and assert their right to self-defense by any means necessary.

RF is an anti-Zionist organization. The Zionist Entity is a settler-colonial project that exists at the behest of the Western imperialists. All of its sins, from the Nakba to the Gaza genocide, can be traced back to British statecraft and American blood money.

As Marxist-Leninist and anti-imperialist organizers from within the belly of the beast, it is our obligation to expose the Zionist entity to as much pressure as possible from as many sources as possible, such that it becomes prohibitively difficult for the media, political class, and economic elite to continue whitewashing its genocidal ambitions.

Finally, RF affirms the efforts of the Palestinian people to decolonize their nation and establish One Democratic State “From the River to the Sea” with Al-Quds (Jerusalem) as its capital.
